A commercial license grants rights beyond MIT, typically including:

- **Private support** — direct access to the maintainers with agreed response
  times and hotfix commitments.
- **Indemnification** — protection against third-party IP claims arising from
  your use of Corros.
- **Redistribution** — permission to bundle or embed Corros in closed-source or
  proprietary products under a non-MIT license of your choosing.
- **Custom work** — prioritized implementation of language features your
  product needs.
- **Long-term maintenance guarantees** — locked versions, backported fixes,
